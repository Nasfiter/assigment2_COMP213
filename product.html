Jeep Wagoneer Series II

The Jeep Wagoneer is a full-size SUV built for big families and long trips. It has three rows of real seats, so adults can sit comfortably even in the back. Its twin-turbo inline-six engine is stronger than most V8s, and it can tow a camper or a boat without effort. It is a comfortable choice for anyone who needs space and power together.

  • 3.0L Hurricane twin-turbo inline-six
  • 420 hp / 468 lb-ft of torque
  • 8-speed automatic transmission
  • Seats up to 8 passengers
  • Tows up to 10,000 lb
